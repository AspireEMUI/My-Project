================================================================================
🚀 NETLIFY DEPLOYMENT - FINAL STEP
================================================================================

Your Netlify CLI is installed and ready! ✅

Now run this command in your terminal to deploy:

  netlify deploy --prod --dir=.

================================================================================
WHAT WILL HAPPEN:
================================================================================

1. Browser will open for authentication
   → Click "Authorize" to connect to Netlify
   → You'll be redirected to confirm

2. CLI will ask where your site files are
   → Just press Enter (current folder is correct)

3. Your site will upload to Netlify
   → Shows progress: "Deploying site"

4. You'll get a LIVE URL
   → Example: https://your-site-name.netlify.app
   → This is your cosmetics landing page LIVE! 🎉

5. Optional: Link to custom domain
   → Can do this later in Netlify dashboard

================================================================================
TO DEPLOY NOW:
================================================================================

In your terminal, run:

  netlify deploy --prod --dir=.

Or copy-paste this to run in Claude:

  ! netlify deploy --prod --dir=.

================================================================================
AFTER DEPLOYMENT:
================================================================================

1. ✅ Your site is LIVE
